Good to know

Questions schools ask.

Is it really free?

Yes. Basic listings are free. Featured placement options are shown after you verify your email.

Do you post our tuition?

No. Students request pricing and schedules from you directly.

How are inquiries screened?

Students answer your required questions (license, start date and program) before an inquiry reaches you. You make every enrollment decision.

Do you verify licenses?

No. Schools and trainers are responsible for their own licensing and compliance, and listings say so.
