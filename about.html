About Seeking Aesthetics

Every aesthetics school in one place.

We help future estheticians, and the parents helping them choose, find and compare schools and trainings. Schools reach students who are already looking.

Why we built this

Choosing an aesthetics school is a big decision. Tuition, program length, schedules and start dates are scattered across school websites, social posts and phone calls.

Seeking Aesthetics puts those answers side by side. Search by city, compare up to three schools, and contact the school directly. It's free for students, and you don't need an account to browse.
