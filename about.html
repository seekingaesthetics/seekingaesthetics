About

Know where you're going.

Choosing an esthetics school or an injector course is a big decision, and the information is scattered across dozens of websites. Seeking Aesthetics puts Utah's schools and trainings in one place so you can search, compare and ask questions without the runaround.

Built in Utah County

Seeking Aesthetics was founded by Melissa Sampson, MBA, a healthcare administrator and longtime aesthetics client with years of relationship-building across Utah's healthcare community.

Free for students

Searching, comparing and requesting info costs students nothing. Schools and trainers fund the platform.

Honest listings

Every listing is reviewed before it goes live. We don't post tuition, and we don't claim to verify licenses — schools are responsible for their own compliance.

Starting in Utah

We're building Utah first, then growing state by state across the West.
